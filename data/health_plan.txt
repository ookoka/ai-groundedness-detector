Company Health Plan Coverage

The company provides comprehensive health plan options for all employees.
Plans cover medical, dental, and vision services.
Coverage includes preventive care at no cost to employees.
Annual health screenings are fully covered.

Health Plan Details:
- Medical: PPO and HMO options available
- Dental: Preventive and restorative coverage
- Vision: Eye exams and corrective lenses
- Prescriptions: Generic and brand-name coverage
- Mental health: Counseling and therapy services

Enrollment occurs during annual open enrollment period in Q4.
